## 1. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. We do not require you to create an account,
and we do not sell your information to anyone.

### 1.1 When you request an appointment

| Information | Required? |
|---|---|
| Your name | Yes |
| Your phone number | Yes |
| Your email address | Optional |
| The branch you wish to visit | Yes |
| The therapy or service you are interested in | Optional |
| Your preferred date and time | Optional |
| A short description of what you would like help with | Optional |
| Your agreement to be contacted about the request | Yes |

### 1.2 When you send a general enquiry

Your name, phone number, an optional email address, and your message.

### 1.3 When you apply for a job

Your name, phone number, an optional email address, the role you are applying for, your
experience, and a short note about yourself. If you choose to upload a CV or resume, we also
receive that document. You may instead choose to email your CV to us separately.

### 1.4 Information collected automatically

When you submit a form we record the date and time, your IP address and your browser's
user-agent string. We use this only to protect the website from automated abuse and spam, and to
investigate problems.

We do **not** currently use advertising cookies or third-party tracking on this website.

> `UNKNOWN — CLIENT INPUT REQUIRED:` if website analytics are added later, this section must be
> updated to say which analytics service is used and how consent is obtained.

### 1.5 Information we do **not** ask for through this website

We do not ask for, and you should not send us through this website, your financial or payment
details, government identification numbers, or medical records and reports. Please bring any
prescriptions, scans or reports with you to your consultation instead.

## 2. Why we collect it

| Purpose | What we use |
|---|---|
| To contact you about the appointment or enquiry you submitted | Name, phone, email |
| To send your request to the correct clinic branch | Branch, name, phone |
| To prepare for your consultation | Service of interest, preferred time, your description |

## 3. Health and symptom information — please read this

The appointment and enquiry forms include a free-text box where you may describe your symptoms
or what you would like help with. Anything you write there is **personal health information**,
and we treat it with particular care.

- **You decide how much to share.** The description field is optional. You can leave it blank, or
  write only a few words, and still request an appointment.
- **We keep clinical detail inside our own system.** What you write is stored encrypted and is
  readable only by signing in to our administration system. 🔴 **This website sends no email to
  anyone (D-038)**, so your description is never placed in an email by us.
- **Access is restricted.** Only authorised clinic staff can view enquiry details, and access is
  logged.
- **Please do not send detailed medical records through this website.** Bring them to your
  consultation.

## 4. Careers and resumes

If you apply for a role:

- Your application is stored in our administration system and is visible only to authorised staff.
- If you **upload** a CV, it is stored privately. It is **not** published, **not** indexed by
  search engines, and has **no public web address**. Staff access it through a temporary,
  time-limited link that expires shortly after it is created. Each access is logged.
- If you choose to **email** your CV instead, we give you a reference number so we can match your
  document to your application.
- 🔴 **This website sends no email (D-038)**, so your CV is never emailed by us to anyone.
- We use your application only to assess you for the role you applied for, and for other roles at
  the clinic if you have asked us to keep you in mind.

> `UNKNOWN — CLIENT INPUT REQUIRED:` how long unsuccessful applications and CVs should be kept.
> Our current proposal is **12 months**, after which they are deleted.

## 5. WhatsApp and other third-party services

### 5.1 WhatsApp

Our appointment and enquiry forms are designed to open **WhatsApp** on your own device with your
message already prepared. **You** press send, so the message is sent from your own WhatsApp
account to the clinic.

This means:

- Your message passes through **WhatsApp**, which is operated by Meta, and is handled under
  **WhatsApp's own privacy policy** — not ours.
- The clinic sees your WhatsApp phone number and profile, as it would for any WhatsApp message.
- WhatsApp is optional. You can instead call the clinic, or email us.

### 5.2 Other services we rely on

| Service | What it does | Where your data goes |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** (Meta) | Carries your enquiry message if you choose that route | Your message and WhatsApp number |
| **Google Maps** | Shows our clinic location on the contact page | Your IP address, when the map loads |
| **YouTube** (Google) | Hosts our Health Talks videos. Videos load only after you click play | Your IP address and viewing data, when you choose to play a video |
| **Cloudinary** | Stores and delivers our website images, and stores uploaded CVs privately | Images; and your CV if you upload one |
| **Our hosting and database providers** | Run the website and store enquiries securely | Your submitted information |

Each of these providers operates under its own privacy policy. We choose providers that allow us
to keep uploaded CVs private rather than publicly accessible.

## 6. How we store and protect your information

- Information is stored in a secured database that is not publicly accessible.
- Access to the administration system requires a username and password, and is limited to
  authorised clinic staff. There is no public sign-up.
- Changes to records, exports of data, and every access to an uploaded CV are **logged**.
- Uploaded CVs are stored privately, with no public web address.
- Information is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Enquiry messages containing health information are protected with additional safeguards inside
  our system.
- We take automated backups so your enquiry is not lost.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. We work to protect your information but cannot
promise absolute security.

## 9. Consent

When you submit the appointment form you tick a box confirming that you agree to be contacted
about your request. That is the basis on which we contact you.

- Ticking the box means you agree we may **call, message or email you about that enquiry**.
- It does **not** mean you have agreed to marketing.
- You can withdraw your agreement at any time by telling us, and we will stop contacting you.

If you contact us by phone, by WhatsApp, or by walking in, we handle your information in the same
way described in this policy.

## 10. Children

Our services may be provided to children when a parent or guardian brings them to the clinic.
However, **this website is not intended for use by children**, and we ask that appointment
requests and enquiries are submitted by a parent, guardian or other responsible adult.

We do not knowingly collect information directly from a child through this website. If you
believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.
